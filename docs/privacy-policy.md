This page covers Pilot Academy, the training site you are using right now.

## What we collect

When you create an account, or an administrator creates one for you, we
store your **name**, **email address**, and the **partner organisation**
you belong to. If you set one, we also store the **name you want printed
on your certificates** (it can differ from your account name) and a
**profile photo**.

As you use the academy, we record your **course and lesson progress**:
which lessons you have opened and finished, your quiz answers and scores,
how far into a video you got, any **course feedback** you leave, and the
**certificates** you earn. If you sign in, this is tied to your account; if
you are not signed in, it is kept in your browser session only and is lost
when the session ends.

We also keep a short **activity log** (sign-ins, which courses and lessons
you opened) and standard **session data** — your IP address and browser
("user agent") — the same way almost every website does, to keep you signed
in and to keep the site secure.

## Why we collect it

To run the training itself: show you the right courses, grade your
quizzes, track what you have completed, issue your certificates, and let
an administrator answer "has this person finished their training yet?"
without asking you directly.

## Who can see it

Administrators at Pilot Academy, and the administrators of your own
partner organisation, can see your learning progress, certificates, and
feedback. They cannot see other partners' students. We do not sell your
data, and we do not show it to anyone outside the academy except where
this page says so.

## Services we use to run the academy

A few outside services help run specific parts of the academy. None of
them are contacted unless the feature that uses them is actually in use.

- **Email.** To send you a certificate, a reminder if you have gone quiet,
  or a password reset link, we send an email through our mail provider.
- **Video translation (Descript).** If an administrator turns this on and
  asks for a lesson video to be translated or dubbed, that video is sent to
  Descript to do the work. This only happens for videos an editor
  specifically selects, not automatically.
- **Text translation (DeepL, ChatGPT, DeepSeek).** If an administrator
  switches one of these on, course or lesson text can be sent to that
  provider to draft a translation, which an editor then reviews before it
  is saved. Your personal account details are never part of that text.
- **Fonts.** The page loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which is a
  standard way of serving web fonts and means your browser makes a request
  to Google's servers for the font file.

## Cookies and staying signed in

The academy uses one cookie to keep you signed in and to remember your
chosen language. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do
not share this cookie with anyone else.

## Certificates are publicly verifiable

A certificate's verification page (reached by its number or by scanning
the QR code on the PDF) is open to anyone who has that number — it shows
your name, the course, the date, and whether the certificate is still
valid. This is by design, so a certificate can be checked by someone who
was given it, the same way a paper certificate can be checked by reading
it.

## How long we keep your data, and your choices

We keep your account and learning records for as long as your account
exists, so your progress and certificates are not lost. If you want your
data corrected, exported, or your account removed, ask your partner
organisation's administrator, or a Pilot Academy administrator directly —
there is no automatic self-service deletion yet, so a person on our side
handles the request.
