## Getting started

**You can start a lesson without an account.** Open a course from the home
page and go. Your progress is then kept in this browser only — switch phone or
computer and it is gone.

**Log in to keep your progress everywhere** and to take the final quiz. Use
**Log in** or **Register** in the top bar. If your company sent you a personal
link, open it — it signs you straight in, no password needed.

## How a course works

A course is a set of **lessons, taken in order**. The course page shows how far
you are, for example **3 / 8 lessons · 32 min left**, and the home page offers
**Continue where you left off**.

Looking for something? **Search** finds courses and lessons by name, and also
by words spoken in a video that has a transcript.

## Finishing a lesson

A lesson is finished when you **pass its knowledge check** — reading or watching
alone does not finish it.

- **Watch the video.** On an uploaded video you can change the speed, and when
  you are logged in it **starts again where you stopped**, even on another
  device.
- **Prefer to read?** Open **Transcript** under the video, where there is one.
- **Take the knowledge check** at the end. Answer **every question correctly**
  to finish the lesson. Get something wrong and the answers to fix are marked,
  so you can correct them and submit again.

Some knowledge checks have a **time limit** or a **number of attempts**. You see
both before you press **Start quiz**. The countdown cannot be paused — it keeps
running if you leave the page — and an attempt that runs out of time counts as
used. These checks show your score rather than which answers were wrong, so
review the lesson before trying again.

## The final quiz

When **every lesson in a course is finished**, the course page unlocks the
**Final quiz**. You need to be logged in.

- Before you start you see the **pass mark** (usually 80%), how many questions
  there are, and how many attempts you have left.
- You type your **full name exactly as it should appear on your certificate**.
  Check the spelling — it is printed as typed.
- Each attempt draws a **fresh set of questions**.
- Once you pass, the final quiz is closed for you: your certificate is issued
  and there is nothing to retake.

## Your certificate

Pass the final quiz and your certificate is made **straight away**: a PDF with
your name, the course, the date, a unique number and a QR code.

- It is **emailed** to you.
- It is always in **🎓 Certificates** in the top bar, ready to download again.
- Anyone can **check it is genuine** by scanning the QR code or opening the
  verification link on it.

Certificates do not expire.

## Telling us what you thought

When you have finished a course — every lesson, plus the final quiz where there
is one — the **Course complete** card on the course page asks whether it was
useful — a thumbs up or down and, if you like, a sentence. Only the
academy team reads it; other students never see it.

## If something is not working

**My progress has disappeared.** You were probably not logged in, or you are in
a different browser. Progress without an account lives in one browser only. Log
in and it follows you from then on.

**The lesson will not finish.** Every answer in the knowledge check has to be
correct — nearly right is not enough. Review the lesson and try again.

**The final quiz is still locked.** One lesson is not finished yet. Open the
course page — a finished lesson has a tick, so look for the one without.

**I have no attempts left.** Contact your academy administrator; they can give
you more.

**The name on my certificate is wrong.** Contact your academy administrator.

**I did not get the certificate email.** Download it from **🎓 Certificates**
instead.
